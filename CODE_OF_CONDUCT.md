# Code of Conduct

This project is a place to learn and build together. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, reviews and discussions) is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect the maintainer's decisions on scope and assignments, and say so on the issue if you
  disagree.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pressuring for assignment, review or merge.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@SakthiQ](https://github.com/SakthiQ), through a private
channel (for example GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, or a direct message) rather than in
public. Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code,
and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
